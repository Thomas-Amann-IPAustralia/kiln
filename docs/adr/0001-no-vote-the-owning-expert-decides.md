# There is no vote: the owning expert decides

There is no vote. The owning expert decides, the chair settles clashes between experts, and dissent is recorded. Only tripwires and changes to what Tom approved wait for Tom.

**Conflict it settled.** The brief asks experts to reach consensus; the Solution, Security and Governance proposals say binding constraints cannot be outvoted.

**Why.** Votes average away expertise and slow the work. Recorded dissent keeps disagreement visible to Tom.

**Decided.** Tom, feedback of 7 October 2026.
