# Obligations are designed in; a deferral needs a reason and Tom

Obligations are designed in by default. Deferral is for disproportion, never for difficulty; it needs a recorded reason, and Tom settles or accepts every deferral at G4.

**Conflict it settled.** The Security, Governance and UX proposals make their checks hard release gates; the second version of this charter made them advisory only.

**Why.** Production quality without blocking the build on every finding, and without using expert review as an excuse.

**Decided.** Tom, feedback of 7 October 2026.
