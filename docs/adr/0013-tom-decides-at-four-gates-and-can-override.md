# Tom decides at four gates and can override anything

Tom sets the question at G0, approves the plan at G2, accepts a test-ready build at G4 and decides what follows at G5. He can override any rule or decision, and each override is recorded. Production follows the agency's approval path with human experts.

**Conflict it settled.** The first version of this charter limited Tom's risk acceptance to MVP work on synthetic or public data; the proposals say "human" without saying who.

**Why.** Tom is accountable for the Council, and production approval stays with human experts, as the brief intends.

**Decided.** Tom, feedback of 7 October 2026; edited after his feedback of 10 November 2026.
