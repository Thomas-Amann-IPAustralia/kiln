# The Council hands over a test-ready build at G4; Tom's team runs the test

The Council hands Tom's team a test-ready build at G4: it has passed the Council's pre-release checks and carries an assurance case and test plan. Tom's team runs the real-world test, and results come back for the next round.

**Conflict it settled.** Earlier versions of this charter left open who runs real-world tests; hosting them would put real users and data inside Council infrastructure.

**Why.** Tom's direction of 7 October 2026. Real users and real data stay with the team, and the Council stays a build system.

**Decided.** Tom, feedback of 7 October 2026.
