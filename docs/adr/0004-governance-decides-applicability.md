# Governance decides whether a regime applies; the domain expert decides how to meet it

Governance decides whether a regime applies, PSPF and ISM included. The domain expert decides how to meet it.

**Conflict it settled.** Solution gives PSPF detail to Security; Security gives PSPF applicability to Governance; Data-E gives PSPF interpretation to Security.

**Why.** Scope and engineering are separate questions, and each now has one owner.

**Decided.** Settled in the consolidated charter of 10 November 2026.
