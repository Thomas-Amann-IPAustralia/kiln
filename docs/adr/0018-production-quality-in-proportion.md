# Production quality in proportion, behind real-world testing and expert assessment

The Council builds working solutions to production quality, in proportion to their use. Nothing reaches production until real-world testing proves DVF and human experts assess it.

**Conflict it settled.** The brief asks for solutions that could go straight to production; Tom has since said real-world testing of desirability, viability and feasibility comes first.

**Why.** A real-world test only means something if the solution is good enough to use, and good enough for experts to take further.

**Decided.** Tom, feedback of 7 October 2026.
