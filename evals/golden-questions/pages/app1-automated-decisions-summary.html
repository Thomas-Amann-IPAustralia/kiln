Plain-English guide: the new automated decision rules in APP 1

Updated recently by a compliance newsletter.

The Privacy and Other Legislation Amendment Act 2024 added transparency rules for automated decisions to Australian Privacy Principle 1. Under the new rules, an APP privacy policy must explain how an entity uses computer programs to make decisions about people.

The rules commenced on 10 December 2024, the day the Act received Royal Assent, so every APP entity should already have updated its privacy policy.

What the policy must say

The policy must list every computer program the entity uses, with its vendor and version number.
